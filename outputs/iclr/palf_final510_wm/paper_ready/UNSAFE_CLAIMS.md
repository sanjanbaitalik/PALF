# Unsafe claims (do not use)

- "independently validated"
- "externally validated"
- "the 98-subject holdout confirmed the method"
- "the LLM prior improves prediction" (unless Tier P3 supports it)
- "NCR is the key predictive novelty" (unless C3 is positive)
- "the sample-size increase caused the improvement"
- any claim that the biomarker is causal rather than predictive-faithful
